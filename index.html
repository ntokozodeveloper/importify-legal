Privacy Policy

Last Modified on: 28th January 2026

At Importify, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled in a safe and responsible manner. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our e-commerce automation services for Shopify and WooCommerce.

1. Introduction

Importify provides e-commerce automation services that help suppliers and merchants sync products, manage inventory in real time, and automate product imports for Shopify and WooCommerce. This Privacy Policy applies to all users of our services, including suppliers, merchants, and visitors to our website. By using our services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide and improve our services. Depending on how you use Importify, this may include:

  • Account information such as name, email address, and login credentials

  • Store connection data from Shopify, WooCommerce, or connected APIs

  • Product-related data provided by Suppliers

  • Usage data such as system activity, logs, and performance metrics

2.a Free Trial Policy

Importify offers a 7-day free trial, providing full access to all features so you can explore how the platform integrates into your daily workflow. At the end of the 7-day trial period, you may choose to upgrade to a paid plan to maintain uninterrupted access. If you decide not to upgrade, your account will be deactivated. Deactivation allows us to manage hosting and storage costs associated with preserving product data and hosted images for inactive accounts.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected data strictly to operate and maintain our services, including:

  • Synchronizing product data between suppliers and merchants

  • Providing marketplace product discovery

  • Providing free image CDN for WordPress users, we securely host and deliver images

  • Maintaining system reliability and performance

  • Improving features and user experience

  • Providing customer support

4. Product Data Handling

Product data synchronized through Importify remains owned and controlled by the Supplier. We process this data solely to enable synchronization, importing, and marketplace visibility as directed by the Supplier.

  • Suppliers retain full ownership of their product data and can manage visibility settings on the Marketplace.

  • Merchants can only access product data that has been explicitly shared or imported from Suppliers.

  • Importify does not claim ownership of any product data and does not use it for any purpose other than facilitating the services provided.

  • Importify does not use your product data to train AI, or use your product data for any use besides storing and serving the product data. Albeit we may conduct manually reviews of the product data, we still don't use your product data.

  • Products published to the marketplace are visible to all merchants, if you prefer to export your products to your distributor / or B2B Merchant, you can disable marketplace and only enable product distribution.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal or product data. Information is only shared:

  • Between Suppliers and Merchants when products are imported

  • If required by law or legal obligation

  • Synced products remain your property and are not shared. Importify does not allow third party access to your product data.

  • Your products may be reviewed by Importify for quality assurance and compliance purposes on the Marketplace.

6. Importify Marketplace

The Importify Marketplace connects suppliers looking to expand their reach with merchants seeking products to fulfill customer demand. While we facilitate this ecosystem, specific data visibility and operational responsibilities apply to each role.

Suppliers

  • Products published to the Marketplace are made publicly accessible to enable discovery and importation by active merchants.

  • Importify does not control, restrict, or monitor which merchants view or import your published catalog.

Merchants

  • Merchants are solely responsible for verifying their chosen suppliers and maintaining direct relationships to ensure order fulfillment.

  • While Importify strives to feature vetted suppliers, we are not liable for supplier product quality, inventory stock levels, or independent fulfillment processes. We do however provide automated product updates allowing you to see when the supplier stock levels have changed and make update the products.

Platform Responsibility: Importify is committed to maintaining a secure and functional marketplace infrastructure. However, Importify is not liable for how users utilize the platform or for standard commercial interactions between suppliers and merchants.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal and product information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. When determining the appropriate retention period, we consider the amount, nature, and sensitivity of the data, the potential risk of harm from unauthorized use or disclosure, and applicable legal requirements.

When a Supplier disconnects their store or removes products from our platform, synchronization ceases immediately and the related data is no longer actively processed or updated. However, we may retain certain information as necessary to comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, or for legitimate business purposes such as fraud prevention and system security. Upon the expiration of any applicable retention period, your data will be securely deleted or anonymized so that it can no longer be associated with you or your account.

8. Security

We implement and maintain industry-standard technical and organizational safeguards to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, accidental loss, or destruction. These measures include, but are not limited to, encrypted data transmission using secure socket layer technology (SSL), regularly monitored firewalls, access controls, and secure data storage protocols.

Our systems are routinely audited and tested for vulnerabilities to ensure the highest level of protection for your information. While we take reasonable and comprehensive steps to secure your data, it is important to note that no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. Therefore, while we strive to protect your personal information using commercially acceptable means, we cannot guarantee absolute security. We encourage you to also take precautions to protect your personal data when online, including using strong passwords and maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials.
